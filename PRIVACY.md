# Privacy and Release Scope

This release contains aggregate educational research data. It excludes raw AOI exports, raw assessment responses, student identifiers, linkage files, individual records, internal notes, intermediate outputs, and unsuppressed poster figures.

Assessment aggregates and day-level gaze rows require at least five participants. Web gaze values and their comparison columns are blanked because daily groups have fewer than five participants and pooled distinct-participant counts are unverified. Session counts must not be added together as though they were unique participants. Assessment participant counts do not validate gaze aggregates.

Blank CSV fields mean unavailable, non-comparable, or suppressed values. The validation report records suppression actions.

Automated identifier scans are limited checks, not certification of anonymity or permission to publish. The study owner must confirm public sharing is permitted by the applicable consent and institutional data-sharing terms before publication.
